## Welcome to Gone Phishing

Gone Phishing is a free educational project about **social engineering, phishing, and everyday cybersecurity awareness**. Scammers are constantly inventing new channels and stories — but the psychological tricks underneath rarely change. Our goal is to teach those underlying tricks, in plain language, so you can recognize a scam even when you've never seen that particular version of it before.

Browse the [blog](/blog/) for articles on how these tactics actually work, or head to [Educational Materials](/resources/) for guides and checklists you can read, share, or hand to a less tech-savvy friend or family member.
