Social engineering and phishing succeed by exploiting trust, urgency, and unfamiliarity with how these scams work — not because people are careless. Every year, scammers invent new stories and new channels (email, text, phone calls, fake job offers, deepfakes), but the underlying manipulation tactics stay remarkably consistent. Our mission is to teach those tactics directly, so you can recognize a scam even the first time you see a new version of it.

This site is written and maintained by [Daniel Baker](mailto:danieljacobbaker@gmail.com). New content — blog posts and educational guides — is added regularly.
