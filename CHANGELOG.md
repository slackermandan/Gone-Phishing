# Changelog

A plain-language log of notable changes to gonephishing.org. Newest at the top.

## 2026-08-27

- Added a favicon (SVG, with an ICO fallback for older browsers).
- Added [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/) analytics: unique visitor counts and general (country-level) visitor location, with no cookies and no data shared with Google or any other third party. Documented in the [privacy policy](privacy.md).

## 2026-08-26

- Published blog post: [Unpaid Toll Text Scams](_posts/2026-08-26-unpaid-toll-text-scams.md).
- Added educational resource: [scam action guide](_resources/scam-action-guide.md).
- Resized and repositioned the toll-scam screenshot for better readability.

## 2026-08-25

- Initial site launch: Jekyll site scaffolded with the minimal-mistakes theme, dark mode enabled.
- Added the anonymous "Submit a Scam Example" page and the first version of the privacy policy.
- Connected the submission form to Formspree.
- Removed a personal email address that had been left on the About page.
